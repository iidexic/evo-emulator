# Gap review (2026-10-09)

A quick pass over PLAN.md, `hypotheses.md`, the experiment records
E001–E010 and the code, looking for things planned, predicted or built
that were never closed out. Not a byte-level audit. Each item says what
was found, what was done about it in this pass, and what is still open.
The recent-literature review (`literature-recent-plan.md`) ran at the
same time; its findings are integrated into `literature.md`, and
anything it changes here is appended under "After the literature review".

## 1. Hypotheses with evidence that was never scored

- **H6 (absorb-loop takeover).** E007 found the one adaptation in the
  standard world is a longer absorb phase (K 64 to 112–127 within
  10,000 ticks, every seed, with or without parasites), then stasis.
  That is H6's prediction in kind, and no note recorded it. Done: H6
  note dated 2026-10-09.
- **H3 (no interior size optimum).** E007 gives the c1 = 0 point:
  length 21 in every window of every seed over 250,000 ticks. No
  shrink, but the ancestor may already be the shortest replicator in
  this ISA; nobody has checked. Done: H3 note. Open: a harness search
  for the shortest working replicator, and the c1 sweep (no date).
- **H2 (producer/consumer split).** Testable from E007's death records
  (`absorbs` and `executed` per organism) without a new run; never
  queried. Done: H2 note. Open: the bimodality query on E007 files.
- **H4 (write protection necessary).** `--no-protect` exists in the
  CLI and has never been run in the standard world. One cheap
  experiment; still unscheduled.

## 4. Record-keeping holes

- `hypotheses.md` has no status index. By convention entries are never
  edited, so every original block still reads "Status. Open" and the
  real status is in the latest dated note. A reader has to scan the
  whole file. Suggested: a short table at the top (H, one-line claim,
  current status, latest note date), maintained as an index rather
  than an edit of any entry. Not added in this pass; it is a format
  decision for the author.
- No `README.md` at the repo root (there is one under `analysis/`).
  PLAN.md serves, but anyone landing on the GitHub page sees nothing.
- E009's PLAN entry says "designed and built, not run" and then
  describes E009a/E009b results; the phrase refers to the E009 search
  proper, which is right but reads as a contradiction. Left as is.

## 5. What the E008–E010 line settled and what it did not

Settled: with a store one pass deep, no per-instruction owner-side
cost rule (full, split, transfer) grades an entered host, and intruders
are placement-limited, so defence has no gradient (E009 reachability
argument, E009b, E010 calibration). Not settled: whether the five-byte
self-scan or six-byte eject is reachable under any physics, because
no rule yet makes an entry survivable and repeated. The reachability
argument (four neutral insertions) is an argument from the harness
cost table, not a measured mutational path; whether that is a known
result is a question for the literature review.

## After the literature review (same day)

- **E008's rule is published.** Jha et al. 2026 (arXiv 2609.10817,
  2026-09-09) "Tape-as-Agent" charges the owner of the executed byte
  and not the executor. Verified from the PDF. E008 ran six days later
  without knowing it. Recorded in `literature.md` 6.1.1, the H13–H15
  note, and PLAN §10. Any write-up cites it and claims only the rule
  under ownership and the negative result.
- **Cluster 4 missed it.** The energy-accounting notes say no
  instruction-level system after 2010 charges the owner of executed
  bytes; cluster 2 found Jha 2026. `literature.md` §6 carries the
  corrected statement; `notes/recent-4-energy-accounting.md` §10 is
  left as written and is wrong on that one point.
- **Store depth is the diagnosis from the other side.** Every system
  where a charged host evolved a defence paid from a budget 4–20
  passes deep; Cosmos at 0.9–1.7 passes froze like evo. PLAN §9 now
  names store depth as the next design parameter.
- **Measure chosen.** QNN over `func_hash` groups per census interval,
  plus MRCA depth and mean pairwise distance from the ancestry table.
  Nothing new in the simulator; an analysis script is needed and does
  not exist yet.
- **Two papers unreachable** (MIT Press 403): Bohm, Zhang & Dolson
  2024 (parasites measured with MODES) and Willkens & Pollack 2023.
  Worth getting through a library before Phase 4 measurement work.
- **One analysis now possible and planned nowhere:** the Lenski 2003
  line-of-descent method on E007's ancestry table for the K sweep,
  which would be the first path-with-fitness analysis in this
  substrate. Added to PLAN §8 Phase 4 as a report item only.
