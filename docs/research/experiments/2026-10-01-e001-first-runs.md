# E001: first runs of the Phase 1 core (2026-10-01)

Commit: see git log for "evo-core: first working VM". Config: `Config::default()`
in `evo-core/src/config.rs` at that commit (world 65,536, patch 4,096, locality
512, income 256/tick/patch, absorb rate 8, upkeep 0.1/byte/tick, cap 32/tick,
store cap 64/byte, p = 0.1/21 per write, q = p/4, bit rot 1e-5, debris decay
1e-3). Seed 1. Ancestor K = 64, E = 16, seeded at world/2 with 100 energy.

Bears on: H5 (upkeep plus decay is enough turnover), H6 (absorb-loop takeover).

## Observations

1. Single-genome harness (noise off, children culled each tick): the 21-byte
   ancestor is self-sustaining. 3,000 ticks, >100 births, energy rides near
   the store cap. The §5.3.3 budget table is roughly right.
2. Noise off, children kept: boom/bust. All offspring land in the parent's
   patch (children are placed adjacent, and dispersal is limited by locality
   512 inside a 4,096-byte patch). Population overshoots the patch carrying
   capacity (~8 organisms at 256 income/tick and ~32/tick demand each),
   crashes, recovers. Children die of upkeep at age 7–11 in the crash.
3. Noise on, first build (no store cap, no bit rot): within 6,000 ticks
   replication stopped and 9 non-replicating absorbers held 3 million energy
   and grew without bound. H5 as first stated is refuted: upkeep alone is not
   enough turnover when absorb income exceeds upkeep by 30x. Fixes added the
   same day: store cap per byte (absorb beyond it is wasted) and background
   bit rot on by default (1e-5 per byte per tick).
4. Noise on, with store cap and bit rot: replication still stops by about
   tick 10,000 (2,002 births total) and the population decays to 2 loafers
   by 50,000. Bit rot kills a loafer slowly (one hit per ~4,800 ticks, often
   harmless). Replicators die in the patch crashes; loafers survive them
   because they never pay for copying.

## Open items from this run

- Dispersal: children need to leave the parent's patch, or patches need to be
  small relative to locality, or income needs to scale differently. Test:
  patch_size 512 with the same total income; alloc preferring free space
  farther away; or a 1D world with many small patches.
- Loafer turnover: bit rot 1e-5 is too slow to matter. Either raise it, or
  make upkeep scale with stored energy, or accept loafers as a background
  population (they do release energy when they die).
- Mutation load 0.1 per copy produced only point mutants; no observed
  parasite yet (the lineage never got dense enough for long).
- Speed: 1.3 M instructions/s with entropy loops on, 13.6 M with them off.
  The per-byte RNG calls for bit rot and debris decay dominate; replace with
  a geometric skip (sample the gap to the next event).
  Done 2026-10-01 (`Rng::geometric`): 20,000 ticks at seed 1 went from 2.1 M
  to 60.7 M instructions/s on the same machine. Same event distribution, but
  a different RNG stream, so seed-for-seed numbers above only reproduce at
  the E001 commit. Qualitative outcome unchanged: seed 1 stops replicating
  by tick 10,000 (2,542 births, 6 alive at 20,000); seed 2 slows but has not
  stopped by 20,000 (5,964 births, 10 alive).
