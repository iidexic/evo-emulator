# E014: cost of intrusion in time, pilot (2026-10-13)

Status: pilot run 2026-10-13 (3 seeds, 50,000 ticks, 15 arms,
468 s on 12 jobs); this record was written from the pilot's output
the same day, after the runs, not before. The protocol, arms,
calibration rule and predictions are in `docs/tasks/T02-cycle-theft.md`
§4, written before the build and unchanged by the pilot except as
amended there; the predictions are H16's. The main stage is **not
run**: the pilot took the branch registered in `docs/tasks/README.md`
("E014 pilot", third bullet), which skips it. Data:
`runs/e014_pilot/` (2.4 GB on disk, compressed), `summary.md` there
has every table.

Bears on: H16 (cost in time selects for an eject), PLAN §5.4 and
§8.3, E009b (the same injection at no cost), E016's design (which
cost arm is live), T03/E015 (the template ancestor, now the carrier
of gate 2).

## Question

Under `--cycle-theft-pct N`, is an entered host graded rather than
killed (`entered_rate_norm` between 0.3 and 0.8 at N = 50), and does
the population hold at every N, so that the main stage can test
whether the cost maintains an eject the no-cost world loses?

## Runs

Rules `n0`, `t25`, `t50`, `t75`, `tax` × genomes `eject`, `inert`,
`resident` × seeds 1–3; 50,000 ticks, 50 copies injected at 25,000,
bins of 2,500 (`analysis/e014_cycle_theft.py pilot`). Tax calibrated
from `t50_resident` per T02 §4.2: F = 0.05006 (share of
replicator-class deaths after 25,000 that were entered, 22,758 of
454,624), r = 1,173.86 milli-units per organism-tick, P = 73. Check:
median taxed per organism-tick under `tax` 1,154.4 against lost per
organism-tick under `t50` 1,137.3.

## Results

**Population holds at every N.** Mean after injection 930–985 in
every arm, final 918–1,027; no collapse at N = 75. Entered share of
replicator-class deaths 4.4–5.3% in every arm.

**The governing measure as built does not move.** The runner's
`entered_rate_norm` (mean over seeds, `t50_resident`) is 1.02; at
`t75` 0.91–0.99; at `t25` 1.01–1.02. The underlying ratio is 0.54 at
`n0`, which says the runner's "entered" and "unentered" sets differ
in something other than the cost (the all-deaths set includes
non-reproducers and the newly born, and an entered death is more
often a long-lived body). The branch rule reads this as "above 0.8".

**Re-read on reproducers, the cost bites with a clean dose.** Over
replicator-class deaths after 25,000 with `offspring` > 0 and `age`
> 50 (about 22,000 per run, of which 17–22% were entered), children
per tick of life, entered over unentered, normalised to `n0` of the
same genome and seed, mean over seeds:

| rule | eject | inert | resident | entered reproducers' debit per tick, median / mean (milli; cap 32,000) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| n0 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0 |
| t25 | 0.953 | 0.969 | 0.972 | 150–230 / 1,300–1,470 |
| t50 | 0.895 | 0.921 | 0.904 | 500–880 / 3,500–4,300 |
| t75 | 0.832 | 0.858 | 0.849 | 1,040–1,650 / 5,500–6,600 |
| tax | 0.994 | 1.024 | 1.007 | 0 |

The seeds agree to ±0.02 in every cell
(`analysis/e014_pilot_reproducers.py` prints the per-seed values). At `n0` the raw ratio on this set is 0.83–0.85 (an
entered reproducer has slightly fewer children per tick even with no
cost), against 0.54 on the all-deaths set. Reproducing hosts run at
the cap: median 28.2 instructions per tick against a cap of 32 units,
starved ticks 0% of life; so a stolen cycle is a lost cycle, and the
cost is in time as designed. It is small because entries are light:
the median entered reproducer is debited 2% of its cap at N = 50
(the mean 11%, a tail of heavily entered bodies), not the 50% the
harness gate sees from a pad that enters every tick.

**The tax control is clean.** `tax` leaves the entered/unentered ratio
at 0.99–1.02, as a cost that does not depend on being entered should.

**The eject and the inert are lost at every N, as at no cost.** Both
test genomes fall below 1% of births within 100–1,800 ticks of
injection in every arm (last > 1% at 25,100–29,300), with no living
body by the next census, including `n0`; the same fate as E009b at
N = 0. There is no separation between the eject's and the inert's
last > 1% tick at any N (`t50`: 25,800 / 25,700 / 25,900 against
25,700 / 25,500 / 25,500). The `resident` holds to 50,000 in 3 of 3
seeds at `n0` and `t25`, 1 of 3 at `t50` (lost at 35,900 and
41,400), 0 of 3 at `t75` (lost at 44,700–49,900), 1 of 3 at `tax`
(lost at 42,500; one to 49,700). Three seeds; a direction, not a
result: a cost charged to entered hosts also shortens the life of the
lineage that enters them.

**Intruders are not resident.** Share of intruder deaths with
`exec_foreign / executed` > 0.9: 7.8–10.4% in every arm.

## Reading against the registered branches

The pilot branch (tasks README, "E014 pilot") is taken on its third
bullet: `entered_rate_norm` above 0.8 at N = 50, resident fraction
of intruders under 10%. The fall-through intruder is not staying
resident, so gate 2 cannot pass for want of a resident intruder, and
the main stage is skipped: gate 2 runs with the template ancestor
(T03, E015), whose parasite is resident by construction, inside E016.

What the pilot adds beyond the branch rule:

1. The mechanism works and is graded. On reproducing hosts the cost
   is 0.90 at N = 50 and 0.85 at N = 75, monotone in N, absent under
   the tax. H16's premise (a cost in time that an entered host pays
   and the population does not) is implemented correctly; what is
   missing in this geometry is the dose, because the std ancestor's
   intruders enter briefly. E016's `theft` arm stands, at N = 50, with
   this as its expected effect size on the std ancestor.
2. Prediction 3 (eject ≥ 1% in ≥ 20 of 30 seeds at `t50`) could not
   have been read by the main stage as designed: the eject is lost in
   under 2,000 ticks at every N, and E009b already showed the same at
   N = 0. An injected ancestor-derived genome at tick 25,000 competes
   against an evolved population, and loses on that, not on the
   eject. Any future injection test of a defence has to inject it on
   the evolved background (the dominant genome at the injection tick
   with the defence spliced in) or at tick 0. E016 searches for a
   defence de novo and is not affected.
3. The runner's `entered_rate_norm` must be recomputed on reproducing
   deaths (`offspring` > 0, `age` > 50) before it is used again; the
   all-deaths version is confounded (ratio 0.54 at no cost) and reads
   1.0 where the reproducer set reads 0.90. Noted in T02 §4.3; not
   fixed in the runner, since no main stage is planned.
