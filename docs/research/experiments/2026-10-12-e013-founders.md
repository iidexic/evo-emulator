# E013: sixteen founders instead of one (2026-10-12)

Status: design and predictions written 2026-10-12, before any
multi-founder run of the standard world with the ancestor alone. E009a
seeded mixes of genomes with `--founder`; no run has seeded several
copies of the ancestor and nothing else.

Bears on: PLAN §9 (founders: a lone 21-byte founder takes a bit flip
about every 480 ticks, E004 seed 10 lost its lineage by tick 1,179;
seeding several founders is the cheap fix and whether it changes
anything after the founder phase is untested), the gap review of
2026-10-09 (§3), and every pre-registered count of the form "in at least
N of 10 seeds" so far, which have all had seed 10 dead.

## Question

Does seeding sixteen copies of the ancestor, one in every eighth patch,
change anything beyond the founder phase? If not, multi-founder seeding
can become the default for new experiments at no cost to comparability
after the first window, and every seed counts.

## Runs

- Arm `f16`: the standard world (code default) with
  `--founder 13325271fc11401605086c5a1845486c11101c130d:16` (the
  ancestor, K = 64, E = 16, as `genomes.csv` lists it), 100 units each,
  the same seed energy as the single default founder. Founder j sits at
  32,768 + 4,096·j modulo 65,536 (`sim::founder_starts`), one founder
  in every eighth patch.
- Seeds 1–10, 50,000 ticks, census every 500, recorded with `--out`
  and classified (default physics, which is the world's physics). Output
  `runs/e013/f16/s<seed>/`. Runner `analysis/e013_founders.py`.
- Control: E007 seeds 1–10, windows 1–2 (ticks 0–50,000), which are
  the E004 p512_far_prop_rot runs.

## Measures

E004's persistence table, E005's parasite table and E007's window
measures for windows 1 and 2 (population and replicator-class bodies at
the last census of the window, canonical genotypes with ≥ 2 exact
births, the leading canonical genotype's share, parasite share), and
E007's absorb-count course: the median absorb count over
replicator-class exact births of 21-byte bodies per 2,500-tick bin,
taken per seed and then as the median across seeds. New: founder
lineages, each organism assigned the founder it descends from through
`births.csv` (`executor` → `child`; founders have ids 2–17), counted
with living descendants at ticks 1,000, 5,000, 25,000 and 50,000; and
occupied patches at tick 1,000.

## Predictions

1. **Every seed persists.** Replicating at tick 50,000 in 10 of 10
   seeds (E004/E007: 9 of 10). Losing all sixteen lineages would take
   sixteen independent early failures.
2. **Nothing changes after the founder phase.** Window 2 medians over
   seeds within ±10% of E007's window 2 medians over seeds 1–9 for
   population at the last census (939), replicator-class bodies (255)
   and canonical genotypes with ≥ 2 exact births; parasite share of
   exact births 0.1–3%.
3. **The absorb sweep is no slower.** The across-seed median absorb
   count is at least E007's at ticks 5,000 and 10,000 (96 and 112).
   Sixteen founders fill the world sooner, so there are more births
   early and the bit-flip steps arrive no later.
4. Exploratory, no threshold: the number of founder lineages with
   living descendants at each of the four ticks, and how many seeds
   end with one; occupied patches at tick 1,000 (E007 follow-up at
   K = 64, one founder: 87–95); the tick the population first reaches
   900.

If 1–3 hold, new experiments may seed sixteen founders and compare with
single-founder controls from window 2 on. If 2 fails, the founder phase
leaves a mark beyond 25,000 ticks and single-founder runs remain the
reference.

## Caveats, known now

- 50,000 ticks. E007 is stationary from window 2; a difference that
  appears later would be missed.
- Founder lineages are traced by executor, so a parasite's child counts
  for the parasite's founder, not the host's.

## Results (2026-10-12)

Run on Linux from 482560f plus the uncommitted E012 counters (no change
in behaviour; the runner checks the E007 seed-1 report line at 20,000
first) with `analysis/e013_founders.py`: 10 runs of 50,000 ticks, 17–38
s of simulation and 69–92 s of classification each, under 6 minutes for
the batch; output in `runs/e013/` (`summary_e013.md` holds the checks;
`absorb_course.csv`, `lineages.csv`, `per_seed.csv`, and the E004,
E005 and E007-window tables), 2.3 GB. meta.json lists sixteen copies of
the ancestor at 32,768 + 4,096·j in every run; every birth traces to a
founder. The E007 control's absorb course recomputed with the same code
gives the record's 72 / 96 / 112 / 120.

### Numbers

Founder phase, f16 (10 seeds) / E007 (seeds 1–9): occupied patches at
tick 1,000 128 / 91 (85–95); population at tick 1,000 926 / 617; first
census with ≥ 900 bodies at tick 750 / 1,500 (medians). Founder
lineages with living descendants: 16 in every seed at ticks 1,000 and
5,000, 7–11 (median 9) at 25,000, 4–6 (median 4.5) at 50,000; no seed
ends with one.

Window 2 (ticks 25,000–50,000), medians, f16 / E007: population 933.5
/ 939; replicator-class bodies 229.5 / 255; canonical genotypes with ≥ 2

### Predictions

1. Replicating at 50,000 in 10 of 10: **confirmed**, 10 of 10.
2. Window 2 medians within ±10% of E007's for population, replicator-
   class bodies and canonical genotypes; parasite share 0.1–3%:
   **confirmed, on the boundary**: −0.6%, −10.0% (229.5 against a
   bound of 229.5; over seeds 1–9 only, 232, −9.0%) and −2.5%; parasite
   share 0.94%. The replicator-class count is a single census
   snapshot, and E007's own window medians of it range from 196 to 255
   over windows 2–10, so a 10% difference is inside its window-to-
   window spread.
3. Absorb median at least E007's at ticks 5,000 and 10,000: **not
   confirmed**. Level at 5,000 (96 and 96); behind at 10,000 (105.5
   against 112). Over the whole course f16 is ahead at 2,500, level at
   5,000, 12,500 and 15,000, behind by 1–6 from 17,500 to 30,000, and
   level or ahead from 32,500 on.
4. Exploratory: above. Sixteen founders fill every patch by tick 1,000
   and reach 900 bodies twice as fast; no lineage fixes in 50,000
   ticks.

### What this says

- **Multi-founder seeding removes founder loss and changes little
  after the founder phase.** Every seed persists (seed 10 now
  replicates), and by window 2 population, diversity, parasite share
  and the absorb phenotype match the single-founder world.
- **It does not speed the absorb sweep; it slightly delays the second
  half of it.** The prediction was that a faster fill means more births
  early and so earlier steps. The first step (64 to 96) does come as
  early, but the median reaches 112 about 2,500 ticks later (12,500
  against 10,000) and 120 about 7,500 later (32,500 against 25,000),
  and the two are level from 32,500 on. Post hoc and untested: the world is split among
  9–16 founder lineages through the sweep, so each beneficial step
  arises in one lineage and has to displace the others, where a
  single-founder world is one lineage throughout (clonal interference).
  The lineage counts are consistent with that and do not show it.
- **Decision.** New experiments may seed sixteen founders when what they
  measure starts after about tick 35,000, or when the control is seeded
  the same way. Comparisons with the single-founder record (E004–E012)
  inside the first 35,000 ticks, and anything about the absorb sweep
  itself, keep one founder or run both.

### Caveats

- E005's parasite test is run-length sensitive: in f16 seeds 1, 4 and
  10 the `pad` lineage is present (808–1,455 exact births) but its
  foreign share over carriers with 2+ children is 0.12–0.17, under the
  0.25 cut, within 50,000 ticks, so the "main parasite" is a 21-byte
  genome there and the resistance columns are not comparable with
  E007's (judged over 250,000 ticks).
- Prediction 2 passes only on an inclusive reading of ±10% with f16's
  ten seeds against E007's nine; the replicator-class gap is within
  noise but it is the one measure that moved most.
- Founder lineages are traced by executor (as registered).
