# B0: replicator density of evo's ISA by uniform sampling (2026-10-15)

Status: run 2026-10-15 (Fable, the night T07 was built; Opus built the
mode and runner, commits `226169e`, `761423d`, merged `52153d0`).
Spec `docs/tasks/T07-density.md`; runner `analysis/b0_density.py`;
outputs `runs/b0/density.csv`, `runs/b0/summary.md` (every cell, the
Clopper-Pearson bounds and the found-bodies list). Line B's step B0
(PLAN §8.4): Dušek et al. 2026's U control (uniform sampling with a
fixed behavioural test) applied to evo, before any GPU work.

## Question

How rare is a self-replicator among uniformly random bodies of evo's
ISA, against BFF's 7.1 × 10^-11 per 64-byte tape, and is the H3
shortest-replicator question answerable by enumeration?

## Design

LEN ∈ {8, 12, 16, 21, 24, 32, 45, 64}; N = 10^6 per seed at T = 1,000
(LEN 21 also at T = 3,000), two seeds; then N = 10^7 per seed for
LEN ≤ 32 since the 10^6 pass took 33 s. Each body alone in the quiet
harness (noise off, 100 units of seed energy, children removed every
tick) in an 8,192-byte ring of 16 patches, default physics. Classes as
`--classify`. `--enumerate` 1–3 bytes exhaustively. 12 threads (the
E016 replay stage was running); 3.5 minutes for the whole sweep.

## Result

**No replicator and no one-shot copier in 1.26 × 10^8 bodies.** Zero
at 2 × 10^7 pooled for every LEN ≤ 32 (upper 95% bound 1.84 × 10^-7
per body), zero at 2 × 10^6 for LEN 45 and 64 (1.84 × 10^-6), zero in
every 1-, 2- and 3-byte body. Inexact copiers (a body that writes some
of itself somewhere) are common and rise with length: 0.09% at LEN 8,
1.0% at 21, 2.1% at 32, 3.6% at 64. Loafers 0.01–0.05%. T = 3,000
against 1,000 at LEN 21 changes nothing.

## Reading

1. The spec's decision bullet 2 is met as written (zero at 10^7 for
   every LEN ≤ 32), and the runner says so: seedless emergence on this
   ISA by uniform sampling is out of reach, and line B goes through the
   cubff fork with evo's rules (B1, B2) rather than evo's ISA.
2. The bound does not reach BFF's number. Zero in n bodies bounds the
   density at about 3.7 / n; here 1.84 × 10^-7, 3.4 orders above
   7.1 × 10^-11. Showing evo's density is *below* BFF's would need
   about 5 × 10^13 bodies (two weeks on this machine at the measured
   22 µs of CPU per body). The result is "no replicator at a density
   BFF's soup would find in minutes", not "rarer than BFF".
3. The U control is not the soup. BFF's replicators arise in a 2^17
   soup in a median 2,453 epochs because tape pairs interact and
   partial copiers compound; U is the floor, not the rate. evo's
   inexact copiers at 1–4% are the raw material such a soup would
   work with. Whether evo's ISA emerges in a soup is B3's question and
   is not answered by B0 either way. B0 answers the cheap question (is
   a replicator common enough to find alone: no) and says the cubff
   route is the safe one for B1–B2.
4. H3 by enumeration: nothing replicates at 1–3 bytes (expected: the
   21-byte ancestor needs `alloc`, `copy`, a loop and `divide`). The
   shortest replicator is not reachable by exhaustion above 3 bytes
   (2^32 at 4), so H3 stays a search question.

## What changes

Nothing in line A. Line B: B1 (reproduce Dušek on the GPU) and B2
(rules one at a time in the fork) as planned, when engine time frees
(PLAN §8.3, decided 2026-10-15). B3 (evo's ISA in a soup mode) is not
ruled out by this result and is the one experiment that would answer
whether this ISA can emerge; it is deferred, not dropped.
