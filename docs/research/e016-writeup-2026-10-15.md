# E016 write-up: immunity by relabelling under a cost in time (draft 1, 2026-10-15)

Status: a draft of the paper-shaped account of E016, written by Fable
from the record (`experiments/2026-10-13-e016-defence-search.md`) for
Derek to read before the `drain` decision. Every number is from the
record and `runs/e016/`. Two things are pending and marked: the lifelong-tax control (T10, E017) and
the closure measurement (T11). Nothing here is a new result; it is the
record read as a paper would read it, with what it can and cannot
claim. Working title below; venue question at the end.

**Working title.** *Immunity by relabelling: what a graded, lifelong
cost of parasitism does and does not select for in a Tierra-like world
with energy.*

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

In a shared-memory instruction world with energy, ownership and a
template-addressed ancestor whose one-mutation neighbourhood contains
a Tierra-style parasite, the only defence that evolved in 30 seeds
over 250,000 ticks was Tierra's own: a relabelling of the copy
procedure's search template and its matching label, two coordinated
byte changes. It arose by tick 2,500 in most seeds and was maintained
in 29 of 30, **with the cost of being entered and equally without
it**; the cost (an intruder's instruction debits half its cost from
the host's per-tick budget) raised the relabelled class's share of
replicator births by about a third (23 of 29 seed pairs above, sign
test p = 0.002) and created nothing. No tolerant defence (an eject, a
reset, a self-check) arose in 150 runs. The immune class is not one thing: by
births 60% of it is the relabelling and 40% is bodies of 32–36 bytes
that have lost template addressing altogether and run straight on,
as the standard body does (T11 §2); of the labelled bodies most have
shrunk the template to one or two nops and carry a second copy of the
label, which closes the search against their own flip, and a minority
stay open to it. The entered share under the cost stays at 43%, and
the class persists through thousands of short-lived origins per run
rather than as a lineage. The path to the first complete
relabelling is short (a median 6 steps after pairing off
break-and-repair), neutral in the two-genome harness at every step,
and favoured against contemporaries in the world (world column above
1 in 19 and 20 of 29 lines); in 34 of 47 lines both changes are
already in the parent's body at the division that made the first
complete child. This is the first defence in a program substrate
reported with the fitness of each intermediate, and what it shows is
mutation supply finding Tierra's immunity in a world where selection
on it is weak.

## 2. The gap this fills

Three systems saw a defence evolve against a costed parasite (Zaman
2014 and Kelley 2026 in Avida and Symbulation, Stringmol 2021 in a
string chemistry) and Tierra reported immunity by label change in
1991. None reported the path: the fitness of the intermediates, where
the coordinated changes came from, whether the defence re-arises.
Lenski 2003's line-of-descent method exists for rewarded tasks and
was never applied to a defence (`literature.md` §6.3.1, §6.4 Q1,
§7.4 Q2). Croft 2024 traced one step back for a cooperative trait.
Escondo & Ferguson 2026 showed replays must be seeded from full
snapshots. E016 was pre-registered to apply all three to the first
complete defence in each seed, under a cost in time (the medium under
which every published defence evolved: a parasite slows its host's
reproduction and never drains a reserve the host needs to run,
`literature.md` §7.2 "Execution is free") and with a matched control.

## 3. The system, briefly

A ring of 128 patches × 512 bytes; each patch receives sunlight into a
pool; an organism gains energy only by `absorb` at its location,
proportional to the pool; pays upkeep per byte per tick; dies when it
cannot; its bytes become decaying debris. Bytes are owned; writes into
another living organism's bytes fail; `alloc` claims a child region,
far placement by default. Every instruction has a cost; the executor
pays. Mutation is in the substrate: write noise and bit rot. No
reaper, no fitness function, no task. Deterministic from seed. Both
parents recorded per birth; canonical (nop-stripped) hashes;
full-state snapshots every 500 ticks (T04). The template ancestor
(E015, T03): 41 bytes, a copy procedure entered by `searchf` on a
four-nop label and returned by `searchb`, K = 127, four-nop labels;
persists only at `--sun 2` under the fixed `alloc`/`copy` edge (T08),
so E016 runs at sun 2 with a same-sun 21-byte standard ancestor as
the second body.

## 4. Design, as registered

Five arms × 30 seeds × 250,000 ticks: `tierra_n0`, `tierra_t50`
(`--cycle-theft-pct 50`), `tierra_tax` (random per-tick tax at the
same mean debit), `std_n0`, `std_t50`. A defence is a genome G,
replicator-class alone, that in the two-genome harness against the
registered probe (the ancestor with the first byte of L1 flipped,
`parasite_jump`, entry offset 22) is either **immune** (the probe's
exact births under 10% of its births against the ancestor) or
**tolerant** (G keeps ≥ 90% of its lone rate while probed and beats
the probed ancestor by 20%). "Maintained": ≥ 1% of replicator-class
exact births in four consecutive 25,000-tick windows. H16 gate 2: a
maintained defence in ≥ 10 of 30 `t50` seeds, ≤ 2 `n0`, ≤ 2 `tax`.
Secondary, the paired reading: structural share in `t50` above `n0`
in ≥ 20 of 29 pairs. Also registered: Croft's look-back, T06's line
of descent with harness and world fitness per step, time-to-loss per
origin, replays from the snapshot before first appearance. The
single-mutant neighbourhood (10,496 substitutions and deletions) was
screened against the same probe before the runs: 0 complete.

## 5. Results

### 5.1 What arose

Of 31,349 complete tierra genomes of the ancestor's length (both
non-tax arms), 31,304 change at least one byte of the `searchf`
template (bytes 13–16) **and** at least one of L1 (bytes 18–21). The
rest of the complete set is the same relabelling in bodies changed
elsewhere too. 95% of every viable candidate screened is immune
(20,741 of 21,935; 29,737 of 30,896). Tolerant genomes: 0 in the
tierra arms, 8 in the tax arm in dying seeds. In the std arms almost
every complete genome is a parameter change (endowment operand);
the structural std set is a pad-placement effect, maintained in one
seed per arm and lost at once elsewhere.

### 5.2 The gate and the paired effect

Maintained structural defence: `tierra_t50` 29 of 30, `tierra_n0` 29
of 30, `tierra_tax` 5 of 30 (every survivor), std 1 and 1. The gate
fails on the `n0` clause. First structural complete genome at a
median tick 1,859 (`n0`) and 1,865 (`t50`), max under 10,000. Share
of replicator-class exact births per 25,000-tick window, medians over
seeds: `n0` 19.3, 15.2, 19.3, 17.4, 14.9%; `t50` 26.4, 20.4, 22.0,
21.3, 20.6%. Paired by seed in the last bin: `t50` above in 23 of 29,
p = 0.0023. Census share after 25,000: 3.06% against 1.80%. So the
cost raises the class by about a third and does not create it. The
share never approaches fixation in either arm.

### 5.3 The control failed as a control

At the calibrated P = 88, F = 0.24 the random per-tick tax kills 25
of 30 seeds (4 in the first 1,300 ticks, 21 in a wave at 35,690 to
54,330) where the targeted theft at the same mean kills none. In the
harness the tax alone lowers the ancestor's lone births from 119 to
93. The two debits have the same mean and different shapes: the theft
falls on entered hosts, about 43% of replicator deaths, at about 66%
of the cap; the tax falls on every body at 21% on average. For a body
whose break-even ration is 95.6% of a full one, an even 21% slowdown
is below the margin where a 66% slowdown on some and none on the rest
is not. The P = 86 arm did the same. This leaves H16's remaining
claim, that being entered rather than being slow raises the
relabelled share, without a control. **Pending: T10's lifelong tax
(drawn once at birth with probability F, paid every tick at P; E017).**
Its pilot (2026-10-15) already says something: with the tax drawn at
division only and matched per organism-tick (F = 0.80, P = 26, mean
20.8% of cap against the theft's 21.1%), 10 of 10 seeds persist to
50,000 ticks at a median population of 210 against the no-cost arm's
189–253, and even a tax on every body at 21% persists at a lower
population. The deaths-share calibration the spec first used (F = 0.43,
P = 66) was wrong because entered hosts live about five times longer
than unentered ones (mean age 708 against 133 ticks). The per-tick
tax's extinctions came in a wave at 35,690–54,330, so 50,000 ticks does
not settle survival; the 30-seed arm does.

### 5.4 A harness result the design did not expect

With no cost, the probe costs the tierra ancestor nothing (119 exact
births entered or not): the parasite reads the host's procedure and
the host keeps its rate, as in Tierra. Under the 50% theft the
entered tierra ancestor makes 1 exact child in 3,000 ticks against
119 alone, where the std ancestor with its fall-through probe keeps
half (116 of 230). E014's "graded, 0.90 at N = 50" was the std body
with an intruder that runs a few bytes; a searching parasite runs the
host's whole copy loop in the host, and at N = 50 the host's budget
goes with it. In the world the tierra population persists in 29 of
30 seeds under it, with 43% of replicator-class deaths entered,
because the cost falls on bodies that lose anyway and a relabelled
minority does not pay it. "Graded and survivable" was true of the
population and not of the entered individual, which is the
condition under which the literature's defences evolved. The
dose curve (record §8.10, run 2026-10-15) makes it precise: the
entered template host keeps 0.97, 0.90, 0.85, 0.79 of its rate at
N = 5, 10, 15, 20, then dies of upkeep at N = 25 and above. The theft
is graded to N = 20 and a cliff from 25; E016's N = 50 sat on the
cliff. This reading goes in the paper as a limitation and a result,
and T13's arms at N = 10 and 20 are the test in the graded regime.

### 5.5 The path

79 tierra lines. The line to the first structural complete genome
is short: median 31 organisms (`n0`), 36 (`t50`); after pairing off
break-and-repair (somatic damage passed on at `divide`, then the
undamaged body again, T06's finding on E007), a median 6 steps, of
which 5 are neutral alone in the harness, 0 beneficial, 0
deleterious. The world column (the last step's child's exact children
per death against its contemporaries in its 2,500-tick bin) is above
1 in 19 of 29 (`n0`, median 1.18) and 20 of 29 (`t50`, 1.21): neutral
alone, favoured in the world, the same size with and without the
cost. Look-back: the first complete genome's parent carries a median
80% of its edits from the ancestor; **the parent's body as it was at
the division that made the first complete child carries all of them
in 34 of 47 lines**; the grandparent a median 67%. The two
coordinated changes do not arrive by the route the design expected (a
label flip that is a viable parasite, then the matching template
change). They are written into the parent's body during its life,
and the first complete child is that body copied. In this world a
living body is edited by bit rot (1e-4 per byte per tick; write noise
lands only on the bytes being written, which are the child's), so
"two coordinated mutations" is one organism accumulating two edits
before it divides, not two generations. Which of the two routes
(bit rot in the parent, or noise in the copy that made the parent)
accounts for the 34 of 47 is not separated in the record and should
be, from `births.csv`'s parent body against the parent's own birth
body.

Time-to-loss: thousands of origins per run (median 6,273 and 5,599);
the median origin has no further exact births; the longest-lived
lose it after about 14,000 ticks; about 30 lineages per run are alive
at the end. The class persists and its lineages turn over.

### 5.6 H17 and the parasite load

Parasite-lineage share of exact births at 50,000: above 5% in 29 of
30 `tierra_n0` seeds, median 22.2%, against std's 1.36%; entered
share of replicator-class deaths 23.6% against the registered 25%
(a 1.4-point miss), std 7.2%. Over the whole run the last-bin
parasite share is 19.2% (`n0`) and 31.0% (`t50`) against 1.4% and
2.2% in std. The template ancestor made parasitism common, as H17
said; the cost made it commoner, not rarer.

### 5.7 Replays

1,380 replays (record §8.6). For the template body the relabelling
arises in 10 of 10 replays from every snapshot and from the founder,
in both tierra arms, with 170–240 distinct structural genomes per
50,000-tick replay; the arising rate is 1 in every condition, so the
potentiation count has no contrast and the result is the §6.1 reading
said a third way: the class is reached from the founder in every
stream. For the std body 62% (n0) and 61% (t50) of potentiation
replays reach a structural complete genome against 50% of background
replays, independent of the snapshot tick: the pad-placement immune
forms are a constant low-rate background, not potentiated. The tax
arm's background replays are all extinct (the §5.1 wave).

## 6. What it means

1. **Immunity is a mutational attractor, and most of it is closed.**
   Edits that break the probe's match and keep the body viable are
   abundant among viable mutants (95% of viable candidates are
   immune), so the class is replenished by supply faster than
   selection removes it, with or without a cost. Selection, when
   added, raises its share by a third. T11 §2 (E016 record §8.8)
   measured what the class is on the 34 most common bodies: 15 have
   dropped template addressing (no `searchf` or an empty template, 32–36
   bytes; 40% of the class's births), 12 of the 19 labelled ones have a
   one- or two-nop template and a redundant copy of the label that
   catches their own flip's search (0 label-parasite rows against the
   ancestor's 996), 6 are Tierra's moving target. So the world's answer
   to a one-hop parasite by search is mostly to stop being searchable:
   drop the label, or shorten it and duplicate it. That is a
   quantitative statement about Tierra's best-known result that Tierra
   never made, and the de-labelling is the efficient-replicator endpoint
   (synthesis finding 1) reached by a second route.
2. **No tolerant defence in 150 runs, under a cost that makes an
   entered individual nearly sterile.** The reachability argument:
   the single-mutant neighbourhood (substitutions and deletions) has
   no tolerant genome; an eject is six bytes; the supply of a k-byte
   specific insertion is of order (10^-5)^k per birth. T11 §1 and §3
   (record §8.9): no single insertion of the template body is complete
   either (0 of 10,752; 752 of its 829 jump-parasite insertions sit in
   the label), and the nearest working eject is seven insertions away
   and makes 2 children against 119, with seven inert bytes costing
   the same. At K = 127 and sun 2 the body cannot afford seven bytes
   of upkeep. The negative on tolerant defences is a margin result:
   any insertion-sized defence pays its length before it pays for
   itself, so no graded path to it exists for this body in this world.
   That is a statement about the ancestor's energy margin, which E015
   set at the smallest viable sun, and the lower-N arms (T13) do not
   change it; a body with slack would.
3. **The Tierra-with-energy premise is not refuted and not
   supported.** The premise was that a graded, survivable cost would
   select a defence that energy charges could not. The world got a
   defence without the cost; the cost did not change which defence.
   The condition the literature names (the entered host reproduces,
   more slowly) held for the population and failed for the
   individual (5.4), so the premise was not cleanly tested at N = 50
   for this body. A lower N for the template body is the untested
   arm, and the memo says whether it is worth running.
4. **No hyper-parasite, one re-read of the screen.** Tierra's next
   step after immunity was a hyper-parasite. The screen data allow one
   check: a replicator-class candidate that makes more exact children
   with the probe present than alone (`b_probed` > `b_alone`, probe not
   stopped early). Over 133,237 screened candidates in the four non-tax
   arms there are 10, all in `tierra_n0`, in six seeds, 1,073 exact
   births between them, each present for under 6,000 ticks. Every one
   has `b_probed` = `p_probed` = 119, the ancestor's rate: a body whose
   own procedure is damaged (11–48 children alone) and whose search
   finds the probe's intact procedure, so both reproduce at full rate.
   That is procedure sharing, not hyper-parasitism (the probe loses
   nothing), and it did not persist. Among the 1,196 non-stopped
   replicator candidates of `tierra_n0` the ratio `b_probed` /
   `b_alone` is exactly 1 at the 10th, 50th, 90th and 99th percentile.
   (Scan 2026-10-15, not in the record; T11's own-flip probe is the
   proper test for a hyper-parasite of a relabelled body.)
5. **The path result stands on its own**: the first defence in a
   program substrate with harness and world fitness at every step
   and a look-back on every first appearance. The finding that the
   coordinated pair is assembled somatically in one body before
   division is new, and specific to substrates where living bodies
   are edited.

6. **Everything measured on 2026-10-15 is a margin result.** The
   template body at sun 2 was chosen as the smallest viable sun (E015:
   1 of 10 at sun 1, 10 of 10 at sun 2), so it runs at break-even. On
   that body: seven inert bytes cut its rate to 2 of 119 (§8.9); the
   theft is graded to N = 20 and lethal from 25 (§8.10); a lifelong
   budget tax at the entered debit sterilises a body (T10 pilot); and an
   income take of 10% kills it even from a full store, while a take of
   5% is worth 1.7% of the drainer's income (T12 gate G2). A body with
   no slack cannot pay for a defence, cannot survive a graded cost
   above a fifth of its budget, and cannot be farmed. Tierra's hosts
   had slack because the slice was free; Avida's because the store is a
   reproduction threshold, not a survival reserve. The honest statement
   for the paper: the condition under which the literature's defences
   evolved includes an energy margin that this world's standard
   settings do not give the template body, and the next experiments
   (T13 at N ≤ 20; T12 rerun against the std body at sun 1, which banks
   600 units; a sun sweep) are tests of that margin as much as of the
   mechanism.

## 7. Limitations to state

One body per ancestor type; one cost level (N = 50); immunity defined
against one probe (the registered flip), so "immune" is "immune to
the ancestor's parasite" and not closure; sun 2 for the tierra arms
(the body does not persist at sun 1); the matched control failed and
is being redone (T10); seed 14 lost its founder in both tierra arms
and counts as a no; the harness grades a step alone with a full pool,
which T06 showed can disagree with the world (hence the world column
is read first); replays uninformative at a 100% arising rate.

## 9. Venue, honestly

Thirty seeds per arm at 250,000 ticks with pre-registration and a
look-back is at the norm of the Symbulation and Avida groups at ISAL
(`literature.md` §7.4 Q5). The result is a negative-with-a-twist
(the cost does not create the defence; supply does) plus a method
result (the path). ISAL full paper is the natural fit; *Artificial
Life* if the control and the lower-N arm make the cost story clean.
The method result and the harness finding of 5.4 are what a reviewer
will remember; the rest is the control for them.
